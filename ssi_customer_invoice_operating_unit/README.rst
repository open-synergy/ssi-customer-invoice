=================================
Customer Invoice + Operating Unit
=================================

Adds Operating Unit support to Customer Invoice: an ``operating_unit_id`` field
on the document, propagated to the accounting entry it generates, guarded by a
journal/operating unit consistency check, and scoped by a dedicated record rule.
